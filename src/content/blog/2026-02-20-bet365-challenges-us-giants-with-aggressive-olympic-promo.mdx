## The Economics of "Bonus Bets"

It is vital for the consumer to understand that "Bonus Bets" are not cash. When a user utilizes the "FOX365" or "NYPBET" code to claim $150, they are receiving site credit. If a bettor wagers $20 of bonus credit on the USA to beat Slovakia at even money (+100) and wins, they receive $20 in profit, but the $20 stake is gone. This is distinct from a cash wager where the stake is returned.

This distinction is where the profitability lies for the sportsbook. They are not giving away $150 in cash; they are giving away $150 in *opportunity*. Mathematical modeling suggests that the average recreational bettor will churn through that $150 without withdrawing significant profit, eventually depositing their own funds. However, for the disciplined bettor, this represents a positive expected value (+EV) opportunity. By hedging bets across different booksfor example, betting on the USA on **bet365** and Slovakia on BetMGMsavvy users can guarantee profit, a practice known as matched betting, though operators frown upon it.
